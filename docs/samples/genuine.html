Method and limits

  • A missing C2PA manifest is not evidence of fakery: most genuine images have none.
  • ELA lights up on edges, text and saturated areas. It points an analyst at regions; it never decides anything alone.
  • The double-compression test sees a second save at higher quality reliably and is nearly blind to a second save at lower quality (the usual platform re-upload).
  • No machine-learning "AI detector" is used: AI generation is reported only when it is declared (C2PA, IPTC, embedded generator parameters).
  • Metadata is easy to forge; contradictions are informative, a clean result is not.
